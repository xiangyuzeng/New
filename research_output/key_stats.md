## Key Takeaways

1. **Volume trajectory is positive** — Mar 4 hit 3,755 orders (highest in 30-day window), suggesting upward momentum.
2. **Heavy discounting** — customers pay ~45% below list price on average. Effective customer acquisition, but margin pressure is real.
3. **8th & Broadway is the flagship** — does 2x the volume of median stores. Benchmark for new store planning.
4. **New store "52nd & Madison" launched strong** — 246 orders/day in first 9 days, on par with top-5 established stores.
5. **Customer acquisition engine is working** — 4K+ new customers/week consistently, with no signs of slowdown after 37 weeks.
6. **Food is underpenetrated at 11%** — potential upsell opportunity (lower discount = better margins).
